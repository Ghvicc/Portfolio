About

Onyango Victor is a founder, engineer and creative working across different disciplines and forms of expression.

My work explores the relationship between systems and creativity, structure and expression, individual identity and collective culture.

Professionally trained in Automotive Engineering, I developed a way of thinking shaped by precision, problem-solving, systems and durability.

Beyond engineering, I explore fashion, art, photography, music, sport, food and community through practical projects, collaborations and ventures.

GhViƆɔ became a platform where many of these interests could intersect — from apparel and photography to sound, football, cultural projects and community experiences.

I am interested in building ideas, relationships, objects and experiences that can continue to evolve beyond a single moment.

GhViƆɔ

GhViƆɔ is a Kenyan creative and cultural platform exploring where fashion, art, sound, sport and community meet.

I founded GhViƆɔ as a space where different forms of creative expression could exist together — from apparel and photography to music, football, cultural partnerships and community projects.

At its core, GhViƆɔ is about creating with intention: what we make, why we make it, who we make it with, and what it contributes.

Rooted in Kenya and connected to the wider East African cultural landscape, the platform continues to grow through projects, relationships and experiences.

“We don't need to add more noise to the world. We need to create things worth listening to.”

Beyond One World

I am building across several worlds rather than remaining inside one.

GhViƆɔ provides a cultural foundation where fashion, art, sound, sport, photography and community intersect, while other ventures allow me to explore areas such as food, hospitality, technical and entrepreneurial work.

The common thread is not a particular industry, but the process of observing, questioning, creating, connecting and building with intention.

How I Think

Culture in Motion

Culture is not static. It moves through people, places, objects, sound, sport, fashion, food and everyday interactions. My work exists within that movement.

Expression & Identity

What we wear, listen to, create, eat and participate in can reveal something about how we understand ourselves and the world around us.

Structure & Creativity

Engineering and creativity are not opposites. Structure can provide the conditions in which experimentation, expression and innovation become possible.

Longevity

Sustainability is also a question of what deserves to remain. I am interested in creating with greater awareness of materials, consumption, usefulness and time.

People First

Projects become meaningful through the people who experience them, contribute to them and carry them forward. Community is therefore not an afterthought but part of the creative process.
