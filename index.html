About

I am Onyango Victor, founder of GhViƆɔ — a Kenyan creative and cultural platform exploring the relationship between expression, identity, community and contemporary culture.

My work sits between different disciplines. Fashion, art, photography, music, football, engineering and community may appear to belong to separate worlds, but I am interested in the spaces where they intersect.

Professionally trained in Automotive Engineering, I have developed a structural approach to problem-solving shaped by precision, systems, durability and attention to detail.

Alongside engineering, my interest in art, visual culture, design and creative expression has shaped the way I understand people, objects, spaces and identity.

GhViƆɔ became the space where these interests could come together — not simply as a brand, but as an evolving platform for ideas, collaborations, experiences and cultural work.

GhViƆɔ

GhViƆɔ is a Kenyan creative and cultural platform exploring where fashion, art, sound, sport and community meet.

I founded the platform to create a space where different forms of expression could exist together — from apparel and photography to music, football, cultural partnerships and community projects.

The idea is simple: culture lives in the details of how we dress, what we listen to, the spaces we create, the games we play and the people we gather with.

Rather than creating for the sake of adding more to the world, GhViƆɔ asks what should exist, why it should exist and who it can create value for.

“We don't need to add more noise to the world. We need to create things worth listening to.”

How I Think

Intentionality

Create with purpose. Choose with care. Question what is unnecessary before adding more to the world.

Collaboration

Different disciplines, perspectives and experiences can become stronger when brought together. I am interested in creating relationships that allow ideas to move beyond individual disciplines.

Endurance

Trends disappear. Meaning can endure. I am interested in ideas, objects, relationships and experiences capable of remaining relevant beyond the moment.

Community

Culture is not created in isolation. The most meaningful work often emerges when people gather, contribute and create something larger than themselves.

Collaboration

Building GhViƆɔ has meant building relationships across disciplines.

The platform has collaborated with creatives including DJ Bravoh, Louis Mulema of ICapture Portraits, and Roy of The Clicks of Life — connecting music, photography, visual culture and creative expression.

A collaboration with the Rotaract Club of Kikuyu also connected creative work with community impact. An original artwork created by Louis Mulema for the club's installation ceremony was auctioned during the event, with proceeds supporting the club's projects.

These collaborations reflect how I approach creative work: relationships first, ideas in context, and creation with a purpose beyond the immediate output.
