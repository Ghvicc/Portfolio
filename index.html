About

Onyango Victor, a founder, engineer and creative working across culture, technology and creative ventures.

Trained in Automotive Engineering, I approach my work through structure, precision, problem-solving and creativity.

My interests span fashion, art, photography, music, football, food and community — developed through projects, collaborations and ventures.

I founded GhViƆɔ as a platform where these interests intersect, creating work that connects people, culture and ideas.

I am interested in building things with purpose, meaning and the ability to endure.

GhViƆɔ

GhViƆɔ is a Kenyan creative and cultural platform exploring the intersection of fashion, art, sound, sport and community.

Founded by Onyango Victor, it brings together apparel, photography, music, football, collaborations and community projects under one evolving cultural platform.

At its core is a simple approach: create with intention, collaborate meaningfully and build work that contributes beyond the moment.

“We don't need to add more noise to the world. We need to create things worth listening to.”

How I Think

Intentionality

Create with purpose. Choose with care. Question what is unnecessary before adding more to the world.

Collaboration

Different disciplines, perspectives and experiences can become stronger when brought together. I am interested in creating relationships that allow ideas to move beyond individual disciplines.

Endurance

Trends disappear. Meaning can endure. I am interested in ideas, objects, relationships and experiences capable of remaining relevant beyond the moment.

Community

Culture is not created in isolation. The most meaningful work often emerges when people gather, contribute and create something larger than themselves.

Selected Work
