About

Onyango Victor is the founder of GhViƆɔ — a lifestyle philosophy exploring intentional living, structural design, and the quiet discipline of sustainability.

Raised between the stillness of a small village and the acceleration of a rapidly modernizing city, he learned early how to exist between contrasting worlds: tradition and ambition, silence and movement, craft and technology.

Professionally trained in Automotive Engineering, Victor has experience spanning lidar systems, automotive craftsmanship, and data management — disciplines that shaped a structural approach to problem-solving grounded in precision, durability, and long-term clarity.

Alongside engineering, years immersed in art collecting and visual culture refined his sensitivity to material, proportion, texture, and form. These parallel disciplines eventually converged into a singular philosophy: GhViƆɔ.

GhViƆɔ

Pronounced Ga-vee-chi, GhViƆɔ is not built around trend cycles or disposable aesthetics.

It explores the duality of identity — the self presented outwardly and the quieter self cultivated in private.

Through garments, collaborative experiences, football tournaments, and cultural storytelling, the brand examines how sustainability, design, and community can exist with greater intentionality.

“A quieter counterpoint to noise-driven culture.”
