About

I am Onyango Victor, a founder, engineer and creative working across different disciplines and forms of expression.

My work does not fit neatly into one category. I am interested in the relationship between systems and creativity, structure and expression, individual identity and collective culture.

Professionally trained in Automotive Engineering, I developed a way of thinking shaped by precision, systems, problem-solving, durability and attention to detail.

Outside engineering, I have continued to explore fashion, art, photography, music, sport, food and community. These interests have gradually become practical projects, collaborations and ventures rather than remaining separate interests.

GhViƆɔ became the platform through which many of these interests could exist together — creating space for cultural projects, creative collaborations, apparel, football, photography, sound and community experiences.

I am interested in building things that can exist beyond a single moment: ideas, relationships, objects, experiences and communities that can continue to evolve.

GhViƆɔ

GhViƆɔ is a Kenyan creative and cultural platform exploring where fashion, art, sound, sport and community meet.

I founded GhViƆɔ as a space for different forms of creative expression to exist together — from apparel and photography to music, football, cultural partnerships and community projects.

The platform is interested in expression with intention: what we create, why we create it, who we create it with, and what it contributes.

GhViƆɔ is already in motion — building relationships, creating experiences and developing a growing body of work rooted in Kenya and connected to the wider East African cultural landscape.

“We don't need to add more noise to the world. We need to create things worth listening to.”

How I Think

Intentionality

Create with purpose. Choose with care. Question what is unnecessary before adding more to the world.

Collaboration

Different disciplines, perspectives and experiences can become stronger when brought together. I am interested in creating relationships that allow ideas to move beyond individual disciplines.

Endurance

Trends disappear. Meaning can endure. I am interested in ideas, objects, relationships and experiences capable of remaining relevant beyond the moment.

Community

Culture is not created in isolation. The most meaningful work often emerges when people gather, contribute and create something larger than themselves.

Selected Thinking

Culture in Motion

Culture is not static. It moves through people, places, objects, sound, sport, fashion, food and everyday interactions. My work exists within that movement.

Expression & Identity

What we wear, listen to, create, eat and participate in can reveal something about how we understand ourselves and the world around us.

Structure & Creativity

Engineering and creativity are not opposites. Structure can provide the conditions in which experimentation, expression and innovation become possible.

Longevity

Sustainability is also a question of what deserves to remain. I am interested in creating with greater awareness of materials, consumption, usefulness and time.

People First

Projects become meaningful through the people who experience them, contribute to them and carry them forward. Community is therefore not an afterthought but part of the creative process.

What I'm Building

I am building across several worlds rather than trying to remain inside one.

GhViƆɔ provides a cultural foundation through which fashion, art, sound, sport, photography and community can intersect. Other ventures and projects allow me to explore different environments, from food and hospitality to technical and entrepreneurial work.

The common thread is not a particular product or industry. It is the process of observing, questioning, creating, connecting and building with intention.

I am interested in what can happen when creative thinking, technical thinking and community thinking are brought into the same room.

There is more to create.
